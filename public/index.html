250

Five-player contract card game

How to Play 250

Goal & card points

Five players use a 50-card deck (2♣ and 2♦ removed). A, K, Q, J and 10 are 10 points each; every 5 is 5 points; and 3♠ is a special 30-point card. There are 250 points in the deck.

Bidding

Bidding begins at 160 and rises in multiples of 5. Players challenge one at a time; the surviving bidder faces the next player. The winning bidder must try to capture at least the bid with their partners.

Partners & trump

The bidder publicly calls two specific cards not in their own hand and chooses a trump suit or No Trump. The holders of those cards are the bidder's hidden partners. A partner is publicly revealed only when a called card is played. One player may hold both called cards.

Playing tricks

You must follow the led suit when possible. If void, you may play any card. With trump, the highest trump wins; otherwise the highest card of the led suit wins. The trick winner leads next. Rooms may use Bidder's Advantage (bidder plays last in trick 1) or Start with 3♣ (the 3♣ holder must lead it).

Scoring

If the contract succeeds, the bidder scores bid + 50 and each unique partner scores the bid. If it fails, the bidder loses 50 and each opponent receives the opponents' captured team points. Casual games continue indefinitely; target games end when someone reaches the selected cutoff.
